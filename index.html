Student inquiry

A grade is a claim about learning.

Select two districts. Identify what each policy treats as evidence, how evidence becomes a grade, and what opportunities students have to demonstrate growth. Then consider: Which student experiences might each approach recognize, and which might it overlook?

Prepared as a teaching prototype. District names refer to Washington State, USA. A school-specific policy does not necessarily apply across its district.
